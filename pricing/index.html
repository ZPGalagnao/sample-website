Questions owners ask

Do I need to know anything about websites, AI or ads?

No. We set up everything. You only send your prices, photos and services, give us access to your Facebook Page if you choose ads, and say yes before anything goes live.

Can I start small and upgrade later?

Yes. Many shops start with Package 1 or 2. When you upgrade, you only pay the difference in the one-time fee. For example, going from Package 2 to Package 3 is ₱5,000 more, then the new monthly fee.

Why is there a monthly fee?

The AI runs every day for you, and every chat and every call has a running cost. The monthly fee covers that, plus keeping the AI updated with your latest prices and promos.

What happens if I stop the monthly plan?

Your website stays online. The AI chat, the AI caller and the dashboard stop until you start again.

Will the AI call people who never asked?

No. It only calls customers who filled your form or ad and agreed to be called. It says it is your virtual assistant, and it stops right away if someone says no.

Who pays for the Facebook ads?

You do, directly to Facebook, from your own ad account. The ad money and the customer list stay yours. Our monthly fee covers making and managing the ads.
