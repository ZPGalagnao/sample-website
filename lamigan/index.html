Questions

Good to know.

How often should our aircon be cleaned?

For most homes, every 3 to 6 months. Offices, stores and units that run all day need it more often. A clean unit cools faster and uses less power.

It's running but not cold. What can I check?

Make sure it's on Cool, not Fan, and look at the filter. A thick layer of dust can stop it from cooling. If it's still warm after that, book a visit and we'll find the cause.

How much is a cleaning?
