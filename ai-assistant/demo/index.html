Questions

Straight answers to what people ask first.

Still unsure? Call (555) 010-0172 and ask us anything.

Are you going to try to sell me a new system?

No. If a repair is the smart move, we repair it. If your system is near the end, we show you its age, the repair cost, and the math, then you decide. No pressure.

How much does a visit cost?

One flat diagnostic fee, quoted when you book. If you approve the repair, that fee comes off the bill.

When can you come?

Most calls get a visit the same day or the next morning. You get a two-hour window and a text when the tech is on the way.

Do you work on my brand?

Yes. We service all major brands of furnaces, air conditioners, and heat pumps.

Should I repair or replace?

A simple rule: if the repair costs more than half the price of a new system, or the system is over 15 years old, replacing may be worth it. We’ll walk you through your own numbers.

Is the work guaranteed?

Yes. Repairs come with a written parts and labor warranty. New installs come with the manufacturer’s warranty plus ours.
