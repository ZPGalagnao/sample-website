Questions owners ask

Do I need a website?

No. The form is inside your Facebook ad, so customers never leave Facebook. We only need a simple privacy page, and we make that for you.

I don’t know anything about ads. Is that okay?

Yes. We make the ads, choose who sees them, and manage them every week. You only approve them and see the results in your weekly report.

Is this the same as old-style telemarketing?

No. It never calls random numbers. It only calls people who saw your ad, filled in the form, and said yes to a call. That is why most of them pick up and book.

Will customers know it is an AI?

Yes. It introduces itself as your virtual assistant. It is polite, short, and passes the call to your team when a person is needed.

What languages can it speak?

English, Tagalog and Taglish. Bisaya is not available yet.

What if a customer asks something the AI doesn’t know?

It only answers from the facts you gave us. If it doesn’t know, it says your team will call back, and you get an alert with the question.

Do you add a markup on the calls?

Only a small one. A 2 to 3 minute call costs us about ₱67: the AI voice is about ₱8 a minute and the phone line to a Philippine mobile is about ₱18 a minute. Both are billed to us in US dollars, so the ₱75 per call covers changes in the dollar rate. The phone number is passed on at the ₱1,125 a month we pay.

Why ₱6,000 a month?

About half pays for the phone line and the AI calls, and the other half (₱3,000) pays for our work: managing your ads, new ad designs, keeping the AI up to date, checking calls and sending your weekly report. In month 1 our work is free, so you pay only the ₱3,000 for the phone line and calls. One hired telemarketer costs about ₱18,000 a month and does not bring in new customers.

Can I stop anytime?

After the first 3 months, yes, with 30 days notice. Your Facebook Page, ad account and customer list stay yours.
