Questions

Straight answers to what people ask first.

Still unsure? Call 0900 000 0172 or chat with our assistant, day or night.

Are you going to sell me a new aircon?

No. If a cleaning or a repair fixes it, that’s what we do. If your unit is near the end, we show you its age, the repair cost, and the math, then you decide. No pressure.

How much does a visit cost?

The check-up is ₱500, and it comes off your bill if you go ahead with the repair. Cleaning starts at ₱700 for a window-type and ₱1,200 for a split-type. These are sample prices for this demo.

When can you come?

Most bookings get a visit the same day or the next morning. You get a two-hour window and a text when the technician is on the way.

Do you work on my brand?

Yes. We service all major brands of window-type, split-type, and floor-mounted aircons.

How often should I have my aircon cleaned?

Every six months for most homes. Every three months if it runs all day, or for stores and offices. A clean unit cools faster and uses less electricity.

Who answers when I message at night?

Our virtual assistant answers right away, any time, and books an open time for your visit right in the chat. Our team contacts you before the visit.

Is the work guaranteed?

Yes. Repairs come with a written warranty on parts and labor. New installs come with the manufacturer’s warranty plus ours.
