Questions

Good to know.

Masakit ba ang cleaning?

Most people feel only light pressure. If your gums are sensitive, tell us and we'll go slower and use a numbing gel.

Pwede bang hulugan ang braces?

Yes. In this sample, braces start with a down payment, then a monthly adjustment fee. The dentist explains the plan at your consultation.

Do you accept HMO?

Some HMO cards are accepted for check-ups and cleaning. Message us with your card and we'll check it for you.

Pwede ba ang bata?

Yes. We see kids from age 2. We keep the first visit short and fun so they're not afraid.

What if my tooth hurts badly right now?

Message us and we'll fit you in at the soonest open slot. If you have a high fever with a swollen face, or bleeding that won't stop, go to the nearest emergency room.
